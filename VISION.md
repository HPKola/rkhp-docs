# RKHP Vision

## A Place to Build What Comes Next

We imagine a future where people do not have to wait for permission to
demonstrate their potential.

A student can become a builder.

A learner can become a contributor.

A contributor can become an owner.

An idea can become an experiment.

An experiment can become a system.

A system can become a product.

And a group of people who started with curiosity can become a team capable
of solving problems they once thought were beyond them.

RKHP begins with intelligent robotics.

Our long-term exploration includes:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Machine Learning
- Robotics
- Computer Vision
- Embedded Systems
- Data Engineering
- Sensor Fusion
- Simulation
- Intelligent Decision Systems
- Human-Robot Interaction
- Research
- Open Engineering

The technology will evolve.

The people will evolve.

The problems will evolve.

Our willingness to learn should remain constant.

---

## The Vision

Build intelligent systems.

Build capable people.

Build opportunities to learn.

Build together.
