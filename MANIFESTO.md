# The RKHP Manifesto

We believe potential deserves a place to grow.

We believe people learn differently.

We believe experience can be built.

We believe failure is part of engineering.

We believe documentation matters.

We believe contribution deserves recognition.

We believe beginners can contribute.

We believe experts can still learn.

We believe technology should create bridges between people.

We believe a résumé should not be the only evidence of what someone can do.

We believe building something real teaches lessons that tutorials cannot.

We believe people should be able to look at something they helped create and
say:

> I helped build that.

So we build.

Not because we know everything.

But because we don't.

We build to find out.

We experiment.

We measure.

We document.

We fail.

We learn.

We try again.

We share credit.

We create space for others.

And we keep building.
