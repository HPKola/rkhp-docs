# Contributing to RKHP

Welcome to RKHP.

You do not need to know everything to contribute something.

RKHP is designed around learning through real contribution.

---

## Who Can Contribute?

Anyone with a genuine interest in the project's technical or creative
direction may contribute, subject to project requirements and appropriate
access.

Potential contribution areas include:

- AI / ML
- Data
- Robotics
- Hardware
- Embedded Systems
- Software
- Research
- Simulation
- UX / Design
- Documentation
- Testing

---

## Contribution Philosophy

We value:

- Curiosity
- Ownership
- Reliability
- Documentation
- Collaboration
- Respect
- Honest reporting
- Continuous learning

---

## No Empty Titles

Project titles should reflect actual contribution.

Someone becomes a module owner by taking meaningful ownership of that module.

Someone becomes a project lead by demonstrating leadership through actual work.

Titles are not substitutes for contribution.

---

## Document Your Work

Contributors should document:

- What they worked on
- What they attempted
- What they learned
- What worked
- What failed
- What remains unresolved

---

## Credit

Meaningful contributions should be credited.

Credit may include:

- Git history
- Experiment documentation
- Project documentation
- Contributor pages
- Research acknowledgements
- Public project references

---

## Learning Is a Contribution

A contributor does not need to arrive as an expert.

Learning, testing, documenting, and improving a subsystem can be valuable
contributions.

---

## Build Honestly

Never exaggerate results.

Never claim an experiment succeeded when it did not.

Never represent a prototype as production-ready without evidence.

Failures are part of the project's engineering history.
